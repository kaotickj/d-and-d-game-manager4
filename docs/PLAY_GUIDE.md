# Running a campaign

## Prepare the table

Create or import the party's saved characters in the Compendium. Use the Forges
for editable sheets, the guided level-up tools for reviewed advancement, and
**Manage Selected Character's Spells** for daily spell selections. The Grimoire
keeps casting classes separate and shows their own spell slots and known limits.

Open **Campaign Tools → Campaigns & Sessions**. Create a campaign, select its
party, then create a session and named encounter. Open the selected session and
encounter to make them the active workspace. Campaign membership, presence in a
scene, and participation in an encounter are separate choices. Add the party to
the scene or encounter explicitly when they are present.

Use **World Desk & Quest Journal** for quests, locations, factions, contacts,
lore, routes and handouts. Link related records to make a campaign reference.
The journal's proposed rewards and item notes are records of your plans; use
the ledger for actual awards, transfers and inventory.

## Play a session

**Maps & Player View** imports a map into the campaign. New maps begin hidden
from players, and tokens begin private. Reveal explored cells and explicitly
show the tokens that players may see. Open the separate Player View on a second
display or share that window. Keep the DM's map desk and journals private.
Calibrate the grid's distance per cell and use the ruler for straight-line
measurements. Linked tokens identify saved encounter participants; moving a
token does not change its character sheet. Annotations begin private and must
be explicitly shown to players.

For an illustrated handout, open the handout gallery, choose an existing World
Desk handout and attach its image. Review the exact player text and selected
image before showing the separate player window or exporting the public ZIP.
Source image files stay untouched, and the managed copy strips image metadata.
Private titles, summaries, notes and original filenames are excluded.

Use Battle Trakker or Encounter Command for initiative, hit points, conditions,
effects, actions and casting. Each combatant is an encounter instance. Damage
and temporary combat changes do not rewrite the library's permanent templates.
Record important outcomes in the encounter log or session notes as play occurs.

Use **Campaign Time & Travel** to preview time changes, travel and rest against
the fictional calendar and upcoming events. Apply the reviewed change to move
the clock. Travel/rest previews do not silently consume supplies, heal the party
or refresh spells. Use the saved weather and travel prompts when useful.

Use **Treasure & XP Ledger** to create personal purses and shared stores. Choose
the source session/encounter, record a reason, and review the money or item
command before confirming it. Transfers preserve totals. Record carried items,
containers and actual coin weight to use the carrying panel. Wallet money
represents value; it does not assume physical coin denominations.

Awarded XP waits in the ledger until **Review & Apply Pending XP** is confirmed.
This changes recorded XP only. Use the guided single-class or multiclass tools
to review level-up choices when the DM approves advancement.

## Look up rules and use the Scribe

Open **Search D&D 3.5 Rules** for attributed passages. Import the approved SRD
sources when you want the broader corpus available offline, or import your own
documents with their source metadata. Inspect the passage and citation before
using a ruling. Custom or unmodeled cases remain explicit DM decisions.

The Scribe opens selected context and generates only when you click its local
generation control. Use it for preparation, narration, advice or a recap draft.
Use **Review for Campaign** or **Review Scribe Proposals** to inspect changes
before saving them. Planning text remains marked as a proposal.

A recap requires saved notes or encounter outcomes selected as evidence. Check
that they describe events that occurred, insert their citations, and review the
complete replacement. Source citations help you inspect support; they do not
decide whether a model's claim is true. Public summaries and player handouts
require a separate review for secrets. A gallery public ZIP contains the
explicit Player text and selected sanitized image; the World Desk text export
contains only the Player text.

## End the session and protect the campaign

Save the session journal and reviewed recap. Each session keeps its own scene;
each named encounter keeps independent combat state. Switching workspaces saves
the old one before opening the next. The active selection is restored on launch.

Choose **Library → Create Complete Campaign Backup** to preserve the library,
standalone scene/combat saves, rules references and managed assets together.
**Restore Complete Backup** validates a copy in a new folder before offering
it for the next launch. Keep the original library until you have opened and
checked the recovered campaign. A creature JSON export is useful for sharing
records, but it is not a complete campaign backup.

During play, open **Run Session** (Ctrl+R) for the party, scene, initiative,
quick rolls and explicit session note appends in one window. It shares live
combat state with Battle Trakker and preserves permanent character sheets.

For supported equipment and conditional combat modifiers, select a character
and open **Review Equipment & Modifiers**. Review the calculation before saving
its profile, then use the Combat Desk's explicit character-suggestion controls.
Saved manual AC and existing encounter snapshots stay separate.
